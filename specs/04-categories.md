# Rule Categories Reference

Defines the 14 categories plus the common cross-cutting category.

## Common

What goes in it: rules that apply across all categories and stacks. Rendered as `common.md`.

## 1. Commands

What goes in it: how to build, run, test, lint. Highest priority for coding agents.

## 2. Project Structure

What goes in it: where things go, module/package layout.

## 3. Conventions

What goes in it: naming, style, idioms, language features to use or avoid.

## 4. Dependencies

What goes in it: approved libraries, versions, what not to add.

## 5. Architecture

What goes in it: layers, boundaries, patterns (e.g. controller -> service -> repo).

## 6. Data & Persistence

What goes in it: ORM, migrations, transactions, queries.

## 7. Interfaces & Contracts

What goes in it: API design, events/messages, versioning, DTOs.

## 8. Error Handling

What goes in it: exceptions, retries, error formats.

## 9. Observability

What goes in it: logging, metrics, tracing.

## 10. Security

What goes in it: auth, input validation, secrets, data exposure.

## 11. Configuration

What goes in it: env vars, profiles, feature flags.

## 12. Testing

What goes in it: test levels, frameworks, coverage expectations.

## 13. Performance & Concurrency

What goes in it: threads, async, caching, limits.

## 14. Workflow

What goes in it: git, commits, PRs, docs.
